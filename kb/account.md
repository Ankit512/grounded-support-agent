# Account settings

## Change the email on your account
To change your account email, open Account, then "Profile", and edit the email
field. We send a confirmation link to the new address, and the change takes effect
only after you click it. Sign-in uses the new email once confirmed.

## Turn on two-factor authentication
To add two-factor authentication, open Account, then Security, then "Two-factor".
Scan the code with an authenticator app and enter the six digit code to confirm.
Save the backup codes we show you, since they are the only way in if you lose the
app.

## Close your account
To close your account, open Account, then Security, then "Close account". Closing
is permanent after a 14 day grace period, during which signing back in cancels the
closure. Any store credit is forfeited when the account closes.
