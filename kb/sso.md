# Single sign-on (SSO)

## Supported providers
Northwind supports SAML SSO with Okta, Microsoft Entra ID and Google Workspace on the
Business and Enterprise plans. SSO is not available on the Starter plan.

## Enforce SSO for everyone
Once SSO is verified, an admin can turn on Settings > Security > Enforce SSO, which disables
password sign-in for all members except the break-glass owner account.
