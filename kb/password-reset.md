# Password reset and sign-in

## Reset a forgotten password
If you forgot your password or cannot log in, go to the sign-in page and click
"Forgot password". Enter the email on your account and we email a reset link that
stays valid for 60 minutes. Open the link, choose a new password, and you are
signed back in. If the email does not arrive within a few minutes, check spam and
confirm you used the address on the account.

## Locked out after too many attempts
If you are locked out after several wrong sign-in attempts, the lock clears
automatically after 15 minutes. You do not need to contact us. Resetting your
password with the "Forgot password" link also lifts the lock immediately.

## Change a password you still know
To change a password you still remember, open Account, then Security, then
"Change password". Enter the current password once and the new password twice.
Changing your password signs out every other device.
