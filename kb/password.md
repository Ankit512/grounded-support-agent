# Password and sign-in

## Reset a forgotten password
On the sign-in page choose Forgot password and enter your account email. The reset link is
valid for 60 minutes. If it expires, request a new one.

## I did not get the reset email
Check spam, and confirm you used the email your account was created with. Reset emails come
from no-reply@northwind.app. Corporate filters sometimes hold them; ask IT to allowlist that
sender.
