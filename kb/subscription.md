# Subscription and plans

## Cancel your subscription
To cancel, open Billing, then "Plan", then "Cancel subscription". Cancelling stops
the next charge and keeps your plan active until the end of the period you already
paid for. There is no cancellation fee.

## Pause instead of cancel
You can pause a subscription for up to 3 months from Billing, then "Plan", then
"Pause". While paused you are not charged and the plan is inactive. It resumes
automatically on the date you choose.

## Upgrade or downgrade a plan
Changing plans takes effect immediately. An upgrade is prorated and charged now for
the rest of the period. A downgrade applies at the next renewal, so you keep the
higher plan until then.
