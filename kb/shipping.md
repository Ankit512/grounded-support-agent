# Shipping and delivery

## Track a shipment
Once an order ships we email a tracking number and a link. You can also find it
under Orders, next to the order, as "Track package". Tracking updates within 24
hours of the carrier scanning the parcel.

## Delivery times
How long delivery takes depends on the method. Standard shipping takes 3 to 5
business days to arrive. Express shipping takes 1 to 2 business days. Delivery
time is counted from the day after the order ships, not the day you place it.

## A package marked delivered but missing
If tracking shows delivered but you do not have the package, wait 24 hours, since
carriers sometimes scan early. If it still has not arrived, request a refund or a
reshipment from Orders and we will take it from there.
