# Refunds

## Standard refund window
You can request a full refund within 30 days of purchase for any reason. Go to
Orders, open the order, and choose "Request refund". Approved refunds return to
the original payment method within 5 to 10 business days.

## Refund after 30 days
After the 30 day window a cash refund is no longer available, but you are not out
of options: purchases between 31 and 90 days old qualify for store credit of the
full amount, issued instantly to your account balance. After 90 days a purchase is
final and cannot be refunded or credited.

## Refunds on discounted or sale items
Items bought on sale or with a coupon are refunded for the amount you actually
paid, not the original list price. The 30 day window and the after-window store
credit rule apply to sale items in exactly the same way.
