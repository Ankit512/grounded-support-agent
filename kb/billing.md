# Billing and payment methods

## Update your payment method
To change the card we bill, open Billing, then "Payment method", and add a new
card. We charge the default card on your renewal date. Removing a card that is the
only one on file is blocked until you add another.

## Find an invoice or receipt
Every charge has an invoice under Billing, then "Invoices". You can download any
invoice as a PDF. Invoices show the amount, the tax, and the last four digits of
the card charged.

## A charge you do not recognize
If you see a charge you do not recognize, first check Invoices for a renewal on
that date. If it is still unclear, request a refund from Orders within the 30 day
window. We do not add fees that are not shown on an invoice.
