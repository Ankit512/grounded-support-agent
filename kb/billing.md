# Billing and invoices

## How am I charged?
Northwind bills monthly on the calendar date you first subscribed. Charges appear on the
card on file. You can view every past invoice under Settings > Billing > Invoices.

## Update the card on file
Go to Settings > Billing > Payment method, then Replace card. The change takes effect on
the next billing cycle. We never charge a new card mid-cycle.

## Why was I charged twice?
A duplicate charge is almost always a temporary authorization hold that drops off within
5 business days. If a second full charge is still present after 5 business days, contact
support with both transaction ids.
